## Source types

- **Type 1 — Pre-decision contemporaneous (2010–Jan 2012)**: HS2 Ltd business case documents, DfT consultation material, government impact assessments, parliamentary debates on HS2 prior to route confirmation, contemporaneous press coverage (Financial Times, The Times, Guardian, Telegraph, local press along the route).
- **Type 2 — Parliamentary / audit / inquiry**: House of Commons Transport Select Committee reports on HS2 (pre- and post-2012); National Audit Office reports on HS2 (2013 and subsequent); Public Accounts Committee hearings; Oakervee Review (2019–2020).
- **Type 3 — Academic case studies**: Flyvbjerg and collaborators on HSR cost performance internationally; UK transport academic literature on WebTAG methodology; journal literature on HS2 specifically.
- **Type 4 — Retrospective**: post-2020 cost escalation reporting; Phase 2a and Phase 2 cancellation announcements; post-cancellation lessons-learned literature.
