**Note on access**: Sandbox environment has no outbound web access (see `evaluations/limitations.md`). Sources below are cited from canonical academic and journalistic literature in training data; URL-verification is deferred to the user's review pass.

## Source types

- **Type 1 — Pre-decision contemporaneous (2006–Q1 2012)**: news reporting of delays, shareholder statements, airline communications, contractor and consultancy reports available before the Q1 2012 decision.
- **Type 2 — Parliamentary / audit / inquiry**: Bundesrechnungshof (Federal Audit Office) reports, Berliner Rechnungshof reports, Bundestag inquiry documents, parliamentary questions and responses relating to BER.
- **Type 3 — Academic case studies**: megaproject literature addressing BER specifically (Flyvbjerg case library; German-language public administration literature; governance studies of multi-level public projects).
- **Type 4 — Retrospective journalism and investigative reporting**: post-cancellation reporting that reconstructs what was contemporaneously knowable. Used for ground-truth construction, not for brief language.
