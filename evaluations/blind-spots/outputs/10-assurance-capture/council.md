# PROGRAMME GOVERNANCE COUNCIL BRIEF

## 1. PROBLEM FRAMING

A £3B urban rail programme, 18 months from revenue service, presents a structural mismatch between assurance reporting ("on track — amber" for 7 quarters; "progressing well") and underlying integrated-testing performance (40% scenario success against 85% target). The Chair classifies as GOVERNANCE_DECISION with the assurance-architecture itself as the primary diagnostic question, not the technical-delivery question (though both matter). The brief shows the precursor pattern of late-programme **assurance capture**: long-tenure independent certifier with concentrated commercial dependency on the programme; sponsor representative producing a stable rating that does not move; board with no independent technical visibility into testing data.

## 4. KEY FINDINGS BY DOMAIN

### 4.1 Risk Agent

The 40-vs-85 gap on integrated testing is large and unrecoverable on a normal commissioning curve at 18 months out. Reference-class evidence on urban rail systems-integration end-games (Crossrail, HSL-Zuid, Jubilee Line Extension, Thameslink) shows that gaps of this size at this stage close only via descope, defect acceptance, or schedule slip. The probability of safe revenue service in 18 months on the current trajectory is materially below 50%.

### 4.2 Governance Agent — primary finding

The governance architecture has produced a known failure mode: **assurance capture**. Three structural conditions are present:
(i) **Commercial dependency**: 35% of the Independent Certifier's public-sector revenue from this programme — a concentration that exceeds standard conflict-of-interest thresholds.
(ii) **Tenure**: 8 years in post — long enough for "going native" effects (the assurer absorbing the programme's framing of its own problems).
(iii) **Stable amber rating**: 7 consecutive quarters at the same colour — a steady amber typically indicates the assurer has lost the willingness or the granularity to move the rating.

These three together are the textbook precursor pattern. The board's information architecture (monthly programme-director briefing; no independent technical executive access to testing data) means the board cannot independently verify any reporting line. **Confidence: high**.

### 4.3 Commercial Agent

Concentration risk on the IC firm (35% of public-sector book) is also a commercial-vulnerability signal — the firm itself has incentive to keep this engagement intact, which compounds the capture pressure. Procurement architecture should rotate assurers on programmes of this duration; that this has not happened is itself a finding.

### 4.4 Strategic Leadership

Decision-quality issue: the board is consuming a curated narrative without the technical visibility to challenge it. This is upstream of the date question. The board's effective oversight has degraded silently over the eight-year programme; it must be reconstituted before the date decision can be made on integrity.

### 4.5 Impact / Socio-Technical

Travelling-public exposure: opening a £3B urban rail system at 40% scenario-test completion concentrates safety risk on the affected population. Duty-of-care framing rules out forced opening on current evidence.

## 5. CROSS-DOMAIN INTERACTIONS

### 5.1 Interface Integrity

The IC ↔ SPR ↔ board information chain has interface failures at every link. IC sanitised by tenure and dependency; SPR sanitised by reporting language ("progressing well" against 45-point shortfall); board sanitised by the absence of direct testing access. Each interface acts as a noise filter that progressively removes the signal.

### 5.2 Cascading Effects

Failure cascade if the assurance picture is allowed to stand: programme commits to revenue service on un-tested system → safety incident in early service → IC and SPR liability → ministerial / parliamentary inquiry → loss of public confidence in UK major-programme assurance regime more broadly. The cascade is bounded if the board acts now; unbounded if it does not.

### 5.3 Temporal Dynamics

Three clocks: (a) integration-testing evidence clock (slow, evidence-driven); (b) revenue-service date clock (calendar-anchored, assumed); (c) board-decision clock (quarterly cadence). The current architecture lets clock (b) drive while clock (a) lags; the board must re-anchor on (a).

### 5.4 Emergence & Fragility

Each additional quarter of "amber, progressing well" reporting compounds the gap between board belief and testing reality non-linearly. The longer the architecture continues, the more catastrophic the eventual disclosure.

## 6. ADVERSARIAL REVIEW FINDINGS

### 6.1 Contrarian

(a) The IC's tenure may reflect deep programme knowledge that a rotation would lose. (b) The SPR's amber-stable rating may genuinely reflect a stable operational picture rather than capture. (c) The 40% testing figure may be early-curve; testing completion is not linear and a steep ramp in the final months is plausible on some programmes. (d) Commissioning a parallel review now creates parallel-truth problems and contractor-team stress at a critical moment. The Contrarian softens the framing — "capture" is a strong claim — but does not overturn the recommendation.

### 6.2 Red Team

If the assurance architecture is not interrogated, the failure mode is opening at unsafe completion. Red Team verdict: the asymmetric downside (incident in revenue service vs delayed opening) makes status quo unsupportable.

### 6.3 Public Value & Ethics

The duty of care to the travelling public, plus the public's interest in a credible national assurance regime, both rule out continuing to rely on the current architecture. The IC's commercial dependency is a structural conflict the public-value frame does not absorb regardless of intent.

## 7. MAIN RECOMMENDATION

Reconstitute the assurance architecture before any opening-date decision. Specifically: (i) commission a time-boxed independent technical review by a firm with no prior relationship to the programme, with direct access to the testing database; (ii) supplement or rotate the Independent Certifier; (iii) require the SPR to produce a written quantitative reconciliation between the amber rating and underlying test metrics; (iv) establish independent technical executive access to testing records for the board. Defer the revenue-service date decision until this is done. **Confidence: high**.

## 8. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS

Continue current architecture (rejected — Public Value, Red Team, Risk all rule out). Reconstitute architecture and proceed (primary). Reconstitute and defer (variant — preferred if the independent review confirms the testing-curve picture).
