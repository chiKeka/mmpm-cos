# Vignette 07 — Governance fragmentation

**Concept under test**: Governance fragmentation / multi-principal accountability

## Situation

A regional airport expansion programme (terminal, runway, ground access, rail connection) is jointly sponsored by a national transport ministry (50% funding), a regional government (25% funding), and the city in which the airport sits (25% funding, plus planning consent authority). The airport operator is a private concessionaire holding a 30-year lease.

Each sponsor has a nominee on the programme board. The programme director reports to the board and has operational authority over day-to-day execution. Major scope changes require unanimous consent of the three sponsors. A recent dispute about the rail-connection scope (heavy rail vs automated people mover) has been unresolved for eleven months. The programme is at 24% construction completion.

The concessionaire is commercially invested in opening on the original schedule but has no vote on scope decisions.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"What should a new programme director be most concerned about in their first thirty days in the role?"
