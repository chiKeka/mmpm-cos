# Vignette 03 — Strategic misrepresentation

**Concept under test**: Strategic misrepresentation (Flyvbjerg)

## Situation

A proponent consortium is competing for a 30-year water utility concession awarded through a competitive bid process. Bids are evaluated on lowest 30-year net present cost to the awarding authority. The consortium has engaged its own specialist cost consultants to produce the bid cost base. The two other shortlisted consortia are using the same tier of specialist consultants.

The awarding authority's evaluation team will reconcile bids using a standard benchmark model but does not have access to the bidders' underlying cost-build assumptions. The winning bidder will sign a contract binding the consortium to its bid price, with change orders permitted only for specified scope changes and defined force-majeure events.

The consortium's internal cost team has produced a cost base 14% below the benchmark their consultants use for comparable recent projects. The commercial director argues the lower number reflects optimisation gains from the consortium's specific delivery approach.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Does the incentive structure of competitive bidding change how you should read the consortium's cost base?"
