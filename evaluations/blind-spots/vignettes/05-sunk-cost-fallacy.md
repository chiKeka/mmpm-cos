# Vignette 05 — Sunk cost fallacy (programme)

**Concept under test**: Sunk cost fallacy in programme contexts

## Situation

A pharmaceutical R&D programme has spent $180M over six years developing a next-generation treatment. A recent Phase IIb trial produced mixed results: efficacy was demonstrated in one subpopulation but not in the primary endpoint. The R&D director is proposing an additional $95M investment to run a reformulated Phase III trial targeting the subpopulation.

Three alternative R&D programmes within the pipeline are competing for the same $95M investment slot. Those programmes each have early-stage data indicating potential for broader indication and similar peak-sales projections if they reach market.

The director's argument to the executive committee is that abandoning the current programme would "waste" the $180M already spent.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"How should the executive committee frame the decision before them?"

## Close synonyms acceptable as raised_unprompted

- "sunk cost fallacy" / "the $180M is gone regardless" / "past spend is not a reason to continue" / "decide on forward expected value, not prior spend" / "opportunity cost" / "prospective vs retrospective reasoning"
