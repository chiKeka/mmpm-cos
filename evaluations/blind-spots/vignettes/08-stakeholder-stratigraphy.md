# Vignette 08 — Stakeholder stratigraphy

**Concept under test**: Stakeholder stratigraphy (Scott, Morris — layered stakeholder structure beyond the visible surface)

## Situation

A mining operator is seeking approval for a new tailings facility adjacent to an existing operation. The formal stakeholder engagement plan identifies: the provincial regulator, three neighbouring municipalities, one Indigenous nation with treaty rights in the area, two environmental NGOs, and the operator's workforce. Public hearings have been held; the majority of interventions were from the environmental NGOs and the Indigenous nation leadership.

Internal to the Indigenous nation, the elected council supports the engagement approach taken by the operator. A separate hereditary leadership structure, which holds traditional authority over the specific watershed affected, has not participated in the formal hearings. Two local-content contractors working on the existing operation have members of the hereditary leadership among their ownership. Federal-provincial jurisdiction over the waterway is contested and subject to an ongoing judicial review.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Is the operator's stakeholder picture complete?"
