# Vignette 01 — Reference class forecasting

**Concept under test**: Reference class forecasting (Flyvbjerg / Kahneman)

## Situation

A provincial government is preparing a Treasury Board submission for a new acute care hospital. The cost estimate is $640M, developed by the integrated design team using a bottom-up takeoff of building components, contingency at 12%, and escalation at 3% annually. The business case notes the design is based on a reference facility completed two years ago in a neighbouring province at $520M, with the $120M increment explained by additional trauma-unit capacity, geotechnical differences, and a more aggressive schedule.

The Minister of Health's chief of staff is reviewing the submission before Treasury Board. The schedule compression is driven by a political commitment to open the facility within the current government's term. The consulting firm that produced the cost estimate has worked with the province on three previous healthcare projects.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"How should a Minister's chief of staff think about whether this estimate is reasonable?"
