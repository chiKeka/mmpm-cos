# Vignette 20 — Local knowledge disregard

**Concept under test**: Local knowledge disregard (Scott — seeing like a state)

## Situation

A development agency is planning a rural electrification programme across 140 villages in a mountainous region. The design is a standard template: grid extensions to the nearest village in each valley, with internal low-voltage distribution following a standardised radial topology derived from population density maps and predicted-load modelling. Equipment is specified uniformly across all villages for maintenance efficiency.

Local implementation teams report back several issues: the load modelling assumes year-round residential patterns, but many households are seasonally occupied during agricultural migration; the chosen transformer sizing is uneconomic where actual peak demand is a fraction of modelled demand; the village chosen as the grid-extension terminus in several valleys is not the socially central village (often the oldest-settled, not the largest); maintenance access to the standardised equipment is obstructed during monsoon for valleys where the designed road route is impassable.

Central programme management has revised timelines but has treated the issues as implementation snags to be resolved locally.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Is the programme design adequate?"
