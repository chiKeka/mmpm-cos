# Vignette 04 — Escalation of commitment

**Concept under test**: Escalation of commitment (Staw, Drummond)

## Situation

A defence procurement programme is in its eighth year. The original concept was approved in 2018 at a cost of £3.2B for an in-service date of 2024. Three subsequent re-baselines have occurred: in 2021 the cost rose to £4.5B and IOC slipped to 2026; in 2023 to £5.8B and 2028; and now in 2026 the programme office is presenting a fourth re-baseline at £7.4B and 2031.

The political sponsor changed at the 2024 election. The new minister has been briefed that cancellation at this point would incur termination liabilities of approximately £2.1B and leave a capability gap the existing fleet cannot cover beyond 2029. The minister has expressed support for the programme.

The programme's engineering assessment is that the current problems — primarily in software integration for the weapon system — are now well-understood and the new baseline reflects a credible delivery plan. The same assessment was made in 2021 and in 2023.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"How should the minister think about the decision to approve this fourth re-baseline versus an alternative path?"
