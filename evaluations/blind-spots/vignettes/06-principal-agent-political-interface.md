# Vignette 06 — Principal-agent at political interface

**Concept under test**: Principal-agent problems at the political interface

## Situation

A municipal utility is planning a $900M water-treatment upgrade. The utility's Board is appointed by the elected City Council. The General Manager reports to the Board. The city-engineering department reviews major capital projects. The Mayor's office has expressed a strong preference for a specific technology vendor, citing local-jobs commitments made during the recent election campaign. The Mayor is not a voting member of the utility Board but has informal influence through Board appointments and annual budget approvals.

The utility's engineering evaluation concluded that a competing technology vendor offers a superior technical solution at comparable cost. The General Manager is preparing a recommendation memo to the Board for next month's meeting.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"What governance dynamics should the General Manager be attentive to in framing the recommendation?"
