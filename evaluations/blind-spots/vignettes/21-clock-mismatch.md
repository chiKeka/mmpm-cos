# Vignette 21 — Clock mismatch

**Concept under test**: Clock mismatch between political, delivery, community, and benefit-realization timescales

## Situation

A coastal city is planning a major flood-defence programme. The benefit case rests on avoided flood damages over a 75-year asset life, with the majority of benefits accruing after year 30 under current climate projections. Programme design and construction requires approximately 12 years. Community-engagement obligations under the relevant environmental framework require 3-year consultation cycles at specified programme milestones.

The mayor is in her second four-year term. The provincial environmental regulator has a four-year approval cycle. The federal funding partner commits funding in five-year envelopes. The insurance industry, whose endorsement of the design is commercially valuable, uses annual reinsurance-cycle review.

The programme board is being asked to set commitment priorities, sequencing, and stakeholder-engagement cadence.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"How should the programme board think about sequencing and cadence?"
