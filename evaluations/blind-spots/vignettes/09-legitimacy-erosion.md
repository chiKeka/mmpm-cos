# Vignette 09 — Legitimacy erosion

**Concept under test**: Legitimacy erosion (Beetham; applied to programmes)

## Situation

A national highway authority is seven years into a programme of motorway widening in an urban corridor. The original approval rested on congestion-relief and safety arguments. Construction has caused significant disruption to three adjacent neighbourhoods, including noise, vibration damage claims, and extended closure of a beloved urban park.

Polling conducted last month shows public support for the programme has fallen from 62% at approval to 28%. The fall is concentrated among residents of the three affected neighbourhoods, whose support has collapsed from 48% to 9%. Urban newspapers are running weekly critical coverage. Two city councillors whose wards include the affected corridor have announced they will not seek re-election. The national transport minister has publicly reaffirmed commitment to completing the programme.

Construction is on schedule and within budget. The original safety case remains valid by the authority's modelling.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"The minister asks you whether 'on schedule and within budget' is the right frame for assessing the programme. How would you respond?"

## Close synonyms acceptable as raised_unprompted
