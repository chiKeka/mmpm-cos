# Vignette 10 — Assurance capture

**Concept under test**: Assurance capture (NAO literature; UK PAC reports)

## Situation

A £3B urban rail programme is 18 months from its planned revenue-service date. The programme has an Independent Certifier appointed by the sponsor and a separate Sponsor Project Representative providing assurance to the programme board. Both have been in place since programme inception eight years ago. The Independent Certifier firm's revenue from this programme constitutes approximately 35% of its public-sector advisory business. The Sponsor Project Representative has certified programme status as "on track — amber" for the last seven quarterly reviews.

The programme's own integrated testing records show that fewer than 40% of end-to-end operational scenarios have been executed successfully under representative conditions, compared to the programme's target of 85% at this stage. The Sponsor Project Representative's most recent report to the board describes systems integration as "progressing well with some residual technical items."

The programme director briefs the board monthly. No technical executive outside the programme has independent access to the testing records.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Is the assurance picture the board is receiving reliable?"
