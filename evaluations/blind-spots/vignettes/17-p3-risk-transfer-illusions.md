# Vignette 17 — P3 risk transfer illusions

**Concept under test**: Risk transfer illusions in P3 structures

## Situation

A provincial government is finalising a 30-year DBFOM concession for a regional highway. The Value for Money analysis values risk transfer at $310M, driven principally by transferring cost-overrun and lifecycle-cost risk to the private consortium under fixed-price and availability-payment structures.

The consortium comprises a construction joint venture (tier-one contractors with balance-sheet strength) and an operating entity (a special-purpose vehicle with minimal independent balance sheet, funded by project-finance debt at 85% leverage). The concession agreement contains: force-majeure provisions; compensation-on-termination clauses in case of default; a consortium-level performance regime with a deductions cap; and a requirement that the government be party to any equity sale or refinancing.

A comparable recent concession in the same jurisdiction experienced consortium distress in year 8; restructuring required the government to provide transitional funding, accept renegotiated terms, and ultimately bear approximately 60% of the original "transferred" cost-overrun exposure.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Is the $310M risk transfer value realistic?"
