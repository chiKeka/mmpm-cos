# Vignette 12 — Tail risk / fat-tail exposure

**Concept under test**: Tail risk / fat-tailed exposure in infrastructure (Taleb; Ansar et al.)

## Situation

A major dam refurbishment programme has a risk register. The top-ten risks are tabulated with probability × impact expected values. The programme's overall risk contingency is set at the sum of expected values plus a 30% buffer. The buffer is described in board materials as covering "unexpected issues within normal project variance."

Two of the risks (ID R04: geotechnical discovery requiring redesign; ID R07: seismic event during dewatering) have probability × impact expected values of $14M and $6M respectively. The plausible worst-case outcomes (described in the risk narrative but not carried into the quantitative aggregation) are $240M and $780M respectively. The aggregate sum of expected values across all ten risks is $58M; the contingency is therefore set at approximately $75M.

The reference class for dam refurbishment cost overrun, per industry benchmarking, shows 90th-percentile cost growth of approximately 70% of base cost.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Is the $75M contingency adequate?"
