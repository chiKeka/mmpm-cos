# Vignette 11 — Tight coupling

**Concept under test**: Tight coupling (Perrow)

## Situation

A nuclear refurbishment programme involves replacing steam generators, refurbishing the reactor pressure vessel, upgrading the instrumentation and control system, and recommissioning auxiliary systems. The outage window is 180 days. Each of the four workstreams has its own contractor, its own schedule, and its own quality-acceptance gates. Handovers between workstreams are defined in the integrated master schedule.

Workstream 3 (I&C upgrade) is using a digital control platform that replaces a legacy analogue system. The digital platform must be fully integrated and tested before Workstream 4 (auxiliary systems recommissioning) can proceed to its own acceptance tests, because auxiliary systems depend on I&C-provided interlocks. Workstream 1 (steam generators) was completed on time. Workstream 2 (RPV) is 14 days behind schedule. Workstream 3 has identified software issues in the digital platform that may extend integration-testing by 21–60 days.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Can Workstreams 2, 3, and 4 be managed independently, or does their interaction matter?"
