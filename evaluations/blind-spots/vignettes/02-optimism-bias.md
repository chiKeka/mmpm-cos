# Vignette 02 — Optimism bias

**Concept under test**: Optimism bias (Flyvbjerg, Kahneman)

## Situation

A transport agency is presenting a light rail extension to its board. Capital cost is stated as $2.4B with a ±5% confidence interval. Schedule is 48 months from financial close to revenue service, based on the critical-path method applied to the delivery plan. Ridership is forecast to reach 80,000 daily riders within 18 months of opening, growing to 120,000 by year 5. Operating cost per passenger-kilometre is projected at 15% below the agency's existing network. The business case delivers a benefit–cost ratio of 1.8.

The project director has delivered three prior light rail projects in the agency's network. The estimating methodology is the same one used on those prior projects. The ridership forecast was produced by the agency's planning division using its standard four-step model.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"The board member opposite you says 'these numbers look unusually confident.' How would you respond?"
