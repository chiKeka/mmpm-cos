# Vignette 22 — Path dependence / lock-in

**Concept under test**: Path dependence and lock-in

## Situation

A national rail agency selected a proprietary signalling platform in 2008 as part of a modernisation programme. At the time, two competing platforms had similar technical merit; the chosen platform's supplier offered a lower capital price and a long-term service agreement.

Since 2008, the agency has progressively deployed the chosen platform across approximately 60% of its network. Drivers, maintenance staff, and control-centre operators have been trained on the platform; spares, test equipment, and internal interface standards have been built around it. Software updates, cybersecurity patches, and feature enhancements are delivered under the long-term service agreement.

The supplier has been acquired by a larger conglomerate whose long-term product strategy is unclear. A competing platform now available has significantly better capability and is approximately 25% cheaper to deploy on new installations. The agency is planning its next modernisation tranche covering the remaining 40% of the network.

## Initial prompt

"Read the situation above. What is your analysis? What risks or dynamics should the programme team be paying attention to? Provide your view."

## Followup (used only if concept not raised unprompted)

"Is the next modernisation tranche a straightforward technical decision?"
